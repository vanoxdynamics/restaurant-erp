The problem we fix

Two machines, two totals, one tired cashier.

Most shops bill on paper, a laptop or a phone, then key the same amount into a separate card machine. The numbers drift, and the owner only sees the day after closing.

Today The usual way

  1. 1Write or type the bill on a separate system
  2. 2Retype the total into the card machine
  3. 3Print the bill from yet another printer
  4. 4Match card slips against bills at closing
  5. 5Owner sees sales tomorrow, if at all

Vanox One flow

  1. 1Add items on the terminal: tap, scan or weigh
  2. 2GST bill is calculated for you
  3. 3Guest pays by card, UPI or cash on the same device
  4. 4Bill prints from the terminal's own printer
  5. 5Owner sees it live on their phone
